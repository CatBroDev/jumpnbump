Jump on the other rabbits. Play solo against three computer opponents, or use the original local multiplayer menu.

Contains cartoon blood. No online multiplayer or persistent saves.

Choose how to play

Up to 4 people share one keyboard. No online multiplayer.

How to play & credits

Solo: choose Solo vs 3 AI to play one rabbit against three computer opponents. Local multiplayer: up to four people share the keyboard. Each player jumps over the log, then one runs off the right edge to start the match.

The on-screen controls send the arrow keys; these are also the solo mode keys. Each rabbit only needs left, right and jump: S and ↓ have no movement action. Clicking a rabbit does not select it. Land on another rabbit’s head to score. Esc ends a round and opens the score table; press Esc again to return to the menu.

Original Brainchild Design game (1998), community SDL2 source and existing Emscripten port. This build adds a solo menu action, touch controls, pause and input safety. The original AI, level and scoring are retained.

Brainchild Design: Mattias Brynervall, Andreas Brynervall, Martin Magnusson & Anders Nilsson. Linux/SDL/AI/SDL2: Chuck Mason, Florian Schulze, Ricardo Cruz, Côme Chilliet and the Libre Games community. Emscripten port: midzer. Browser integration: Old Games Archive.
